# Mobile Tycoon

Run your own smartphone company. Research technology, design phones, manage your
factory and marketing — and outsell 3 AI rivals (VoltEdge, PocketPal, NovaLabs).

## How to play

- **Dashboard** — cash, valuation, market share, revenue trend, news feed.
- **R&D** — research Processor, Camera, Battery, Display and Design from level 1 to 10.
- **Factory** — expand capacity, hire/fire workers, run marketing campaigns.
- **Phones** — design phones (specs, price), launch them, discontinue flops.
- **Market** — live phone rankings and company standings.
- Tap **Next Month** to simulate one month. Reach **$1B valuation** or
  **50% market share** to win. Go bankrupt and it's game over.

## Build

GitHub Actions builds signed release APK + AAB on every push to `main`
(Flutter 3.47.6). Signing secrets: `KEYSTORE_BASE64`, `KEYSTORE_PASSWORD`,
`KEY_ALIAS`, `KEY_PASSWORD`.
